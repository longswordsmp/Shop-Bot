1. WHAT MAKES IT PROFESSIONAL (the rules every message follows)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

One message = one card.
  Banner picture on top, then ONE embed. Sections inside the card are separated by a bold heading with one
  small custom icon. No stacks of small embeds.

Card anatomy (always in this order):
  Title           short, no emoji, e.g. "Order #1042 · Payment submitted"
  First line      one sentence that says what this is and what to do now
  Sections        bold heading with one icon, then 1-4 short lines or a compact table
  Details         key facts as fields (Order number, Total, Status) where the reader scans
  Footer          "AmazonYaarts" plus the date and time on every order and status message

Icons, not emoji spam.
  Our own icon set (section 9): flat, rounded, in the brand colours (orange #FF9900, blue #146EB4, navy #232F3E).
  At most one icon per heading or status line. Never inside a sentence. No 🔥🎉✨ decoration.

Colours of the card edge:
  orange = panels and next steps · blue = information and status · green = success (paid, shipped, delivered)
  red = errors, cancelled, rejected · navy = closed and archived

Voice:
  Calm, short and specific, like a good online store's help page. "We" and "you". No exclamation marks, no hype
  ("amazing", "insane deal"). Amounts always exact ($42.00). Every message ends with the next step.

Privacy:
  Addresses and gift card codes are only ever typed into private forms and shown only to staff who need them,
  never in a channel, never in logs.

Buttons:
  Verb first, 1-3 words ("Verify", "Start order", "Submit gift card"). Discord only allows blurple, green, red and
  grey buttons: blurple = main action, green = confirm, red = cancel or destructive, grey = secondary.

Channel names: one emoji + thin dot + name (your choice), every channel has a one-line topic.

3. THE WHOLE JOURNEY AT A GLANCE
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 1  Joins          sees only INFORMATION; gets a welcome mention in #welcome that disappears after 30 seconds
 2  Verifies       reads the rules, presses Verify → Member role → SHOP and ORDERS appear
 3  Browses        #catalog cards (price, availability, Buy), #shipping (countries, prices), #faq
 4  Starts order   Buy on an item or Start order in #place-order → private order ticket
 5  Items          chooses items and quantities in the ticket's live order panel
 6  Address        picks the country (only countries we ship to), fills in the address form
 7  Delivery       Standard $5.00 or Expedited $15.00; extras: Shipping protection $3.00, Discreet packaging $2.00
 8  Review         full receipt: items, shipping, extras, total, address → Confirm order
 9  Payment        buys a DoorDash eGift card for exactly the total, enters the code in a private form
10  Order placed   automatic message: order placed, payment being verified, tracking number will be posted here
11  Verified       staff redeem the card → "Payment confirmed"
12  Shipped        staff add carrier + tracking number → posted in the ticket and sent by DM
13  Delivered      staff mark delivered (or the customer confirms) → Customer role
14  Review         7 days later: "How was your order?" → review in #reviews
15  After          /myorders any time; Report a problem in #place-order; ticket closes with a transcript

13. BEFORE WE BUILD: THINGS YOU SHOULD KNOW
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Gift card payment looks like a scam to many buyers. The US Federal Trade Commission tells consumers that
     "no real business … will ever tell you to buy a gift card to pay them". DoorDash's terms say gift cards may not
     be resold and are redeemed into your own account; DoorDash can deactivate cards it suspects. You also end up
     with DoorDash credit, not money, and a stolen or already-used card can be reversed after you ship.
     My recommendation: add a normal payment option (Stripe, PayPal or Square payment link) next to the gift card,
     and keep the gift card flow strict (only DoorDash eGift cards, redeem before shipping, never ship before the
     card is redeemed). If you keep gift cards only, the plan above is the safest way to do it.
  2. The banners show Amazon's logo and the "amazon" wordmark. Using another company's logo can make customers think
     you are Amazon or partnered with Amazon, and can get the server reported. A version of the banners without
     the Amazon logo would be safer.
  3. Cuba, Russia and Belarus: shipping to them is restricted by sanctions and most carriers don't accept parcels.
     "Every country in the Americas" includes Cuba; I suggest listing Cuba (and Russia/Belarus, which are not on the
     Europe list above) as not available unless you know your carrier delivers there.
  4. Shipping protection and Discreet packaging are promises to customers: the text in 4.5 and 5.5 must match what
     you really do.
